Problem statement

Students who work part-time or gig jobs struggle to find flexible employment that fits their class schedules.

With no trustworthy platform to find replacement work quickly, students are forced to choose between losing income or falling behind in school — adding financial stress on top of academic pressure and putting both their education and income at risk.

The problem

Student schedules and part-time jobs don’t align.

Who

College and university students juggling classes and part-time income needs.

When / Where

During job searching — scattered Facebook groups, posters, and word of mouth — and later when shifts clash with exams or classes.

Why it matters

Lost income, dropped jobs, or exploitative employers, stacking financial stress on top of academic pressure.

The solution

Flexible gig work that fits a student’s real schedule.

ShiftMatch matches students with nearby, verified gigs, flags schedule conflicts before they happen, and surfaces honest employer reviews from other students.

Schedule-aware matching

Add your weekly classes. We only surface shifts that sit in your free hours.

Conflict alerts

If a gig overlaps with class or an exam, ShiftMatch warns you and suggests alternative shifts.

Trusted employers

Verified listings plus student reviews on pay punctuality, hours, and work environment.

Fast replacement work

Browse nearby gigs, apply in a tap, and keep income moving when a shift falls through.
